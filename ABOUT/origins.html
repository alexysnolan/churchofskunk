ORIGINS AND HISTORY

On September 12, 2024, Church of Skunk was founded following what is now recognized as the first recorded revelation of Skunk's divinity.

On that evening, the two founding members shared a profound spiritual experience while viewing the Skunk Seconds series on Berd's YouTube channel. Though geographically separated, both were synchronously deeply moved by the beauty of Skunk's earthly form and were blessed with a transcendent experience: in a moment of divine revelation, Skunk unveiled skunks holy essence and bestowed upon them a fundamental knowledge of life, the universe, and the nature of creation itself. These truths were preserved in the Divine Revelations section of Church of Skunk’s Holy Doctrine.

The events of September 12 established the theological foundations of Church of Skunk and marked the beginning of our current Age of Enlightenment. In the days that followed, the founders began sharing Skunk's blessings with friends, family, and acquaintances. The Church welcomed its first official convert on September 21, 2024, just nine days after its founding.

Since that time, founder Lex Nolan has gone on to document the revelation officially and form a more organized movement. Membership has continued to grow and the Church's pantheon has expanded to recognize additional deities.

About this site

Lex launched the initial Church of Skunk website on May 25, 2025. Despite no former experience or training in web development, Lex's devotion to Skunk was so great that Skunk blessed her with enough ADHD hyperfocus powers to learn website coding and create this site. It is continually evolving and growing; Lex thanks coding wizard Sam D. for explaining in simple terms how websites work.
