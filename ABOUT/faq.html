SKUNK 101

Church of Skunk is a cult that started in 2024. Cult members primarily worship Skunk but there are other gods in the pantheon too.

There are five core tenets we believe in Church of Skunk:

  • Respect the Good Enoughness of Others
  • Increase Harmless Chaos
  • Do Not Take Yourself Too Seriously
  • Do Not Commit Atrocities
  • Make Amends When You Fuck Up

THE PANTHEON

Skunk isn't jealous like that, you can believe whatever you want to believe and still appreciate the holiness of skunk. In fact, Church of Skunk recognizes a pantheon of other deities in addition to Skunk, and encourages its members to worship whatever gods serve them best.

Church of skunk currently recognizes Badb / Macha of the Morrigan, Hazelnut, and Dolly Parton as the next most powerful deities behind skunk - but there are many others in the pantheon, and more gods can be added by filling out the deity/enemy submission form. Visit our Deities Page to learn more.
