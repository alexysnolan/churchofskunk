August

August 20: St Philiberts Day. This holiday celebrates the ripening of hazelnuts (AKA Philberts). Historically, August 20 was considered the date on which hazelnuts began ripening.

September

September 12: 2 Year Anniversary of Church of Skunk!

September 12: Annual Skunk Haven SkunkFest

September 14: Holy Cross Day / Nutting Day. Holy Cross Day was traditionally given as a school holiday for children to go nutting, aka harvesting hazelnuts - a custom which persisted in England until the First World War. Church of Skunk members are encouraged to go nutting in celebration of Hazelnut.

October

October 31 - November 1: Samhain. Badb is connected to Samhain, which occurs annually on October 31 and November 1. During this time, the boundary between the living world and the Otherworld is at its weakest. It is traditional to honor the dead by placing their photo on Badb's altar and lighting a candle. Others celebrate by attempting divination or lighting bonfires.

November

November 13: Skunk Day. Skunk Day is a holiday created by our youngest cult member. It is celebrated by wearing black and white clothing and playing pranks all day.
